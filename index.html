Welcome to AM Counselling Services

Based in Belfast with therapist Alice Magee, committed to providing a professional high-quality counselling service with 8 years of counselling experience working with adults and young people age 12 and above.

I understand it can be hard taking the first step in starting counselling. I offer a safe and confidential talking therapy in a relaxed and non-judgmental manner. Sometimes life doesn't always go the way we want it to and things can happen that we have no control over which can stop us in our tracks. Difficulties can occur sometimes such as broken-down relationships, work life balance or overload, financial difficulties, the loss of a loved one, health issues, addiction, social anxiety and other personal issues, all causing huge stress, anxiety and low mood.

Counselling can offer you a confidential safe space to explore any issue you may be facing and help you to work through your difficulties. By engaging with the therapeutic process, counselling can provide you with the opportunity to learn more about yourself and increase your self-awareness and learning coping strategies can help reduce stress and anxiety.
